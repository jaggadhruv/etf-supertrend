Reading the backtest. Expectancy is the average result per trade in R (risk units); above 0 means an edge. With a 2R target you break even at a 33.3% win rate. Profit factor is gross wins over gross losses. Max DD is the worst peak-to-trough run of the cumulative R curve. System CAGR compounds 1% account risk per trade, so it is comparable to buy & hold CAGR. "No-filter exp." is the same rules without the trend filter, to show whether the filter earns its keep. Prices are dividend-adjusted, no commissions or slippage. Stops and targets assume the level fills except on gaps.
